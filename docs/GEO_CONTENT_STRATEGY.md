# GEO Content & Editorial Strategy — `https://loc.digital`

**Author & Subject Matter Owner:** Phuc Loc Nguyen (Loc / Henry)  
**Primary Audiences:** Vietnamese practitioners/travelers & international readers researching Vietnam travel and performance marketing  
**Core Rule:** Publish only verified first-hand observations, verified client/project metrics (respecting confidentiality), and rigorous mathematical definitions. Never fabricate experiences, coordinates, prices, or client outcomes.

---

## 1. Dual-Pillar Positioning

`loc.digital` connects one real personal brand across two complementary domains:
1. **Pillar A — Travel & Photography (`/photos`, `/photos/[slug]`):** First-hand photographic journeys and location notes across Vietnam and Southeast Asia, grounded in original images, exact capture timestamps, personal perspective, practical planning notes, and clearly separated historical references.
2. **Pillar B — Growth & Performance Marketing (`/growth`, `/growth/metrics`, `/work`, `/services`):** Experience-backed paid acquisition methodologies, TikTok Shop GMV mechanics, unit economics formulas, technical SEO roadmaps, and real campaign case studies.

1. **Cluster A — Meta Ads Scaling:** Campaign structure, creative hook testing, horizontal vs. vertical scaling, funnel segmentation, delivery diagnostics, attribution & CAPI deduplication, fatigue monitoring.

5. **Cluster E — Growth Architecture:** Full-funnel CRO, GA4 server-side instrumentation, technical SEO roadmaps (`pops-worldwide`), CRM lifecycle automation (`tomato-childrens-home`), and AI-assisted ops (`sony`, `playah`).
