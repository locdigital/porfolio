# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, collaborators, clients, and readers visiting the personal portfolio of Phuc Loc Nguyen. They scan work, writing, services, gear, and photography to understand taste, credibility, and working style.

## Product Purpose

loc.digital is a personal portfolio and editorial website. It presents performance marketing work, writing, travel photography, and CMS-managed content with a polished editorial identity.

## Brand Personality

Warm, editorial, careful.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS templates, oversized marketing cards, AI-purple gradients, decorative metadata, and per-page manual styling that bypasses shared design tokens.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the existing warm-editorial identity.
- Let typography and photography carry the page before adding decoration.
- Keep public pages spacious and CMS surfaces compact.
- Fix inconsistency at the token, primitive, or component level.
- Prioritize readable prose with comfortable measure and rhythm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, semantic HTML, responsive layouts, and zoom-friendly typography.
