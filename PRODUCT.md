# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, founders, marketing leads, collaborators, and peers who want to quickly understand Loc's performance marketing experience, proof of work, writing, services, and creative practice.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Nguyen Phuc Loc as a senior performance marketer who connects paid media, SEO, automation, and creative execution into measurable growth. Success means visitors can understand the positioning within one screen, trust the proof, and find a next action without friction.

## Brand Personality

Precise, editorial, growth-minded. The site should feel compact and confident: more like a sharp working notebook than a generic SaaS landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI landing-page patterns, repeated numbered section scaffolds, heavy glass cards, oversized rounded panels, low-contrast muted text, decorative effects that hide the actual work, and portfolio pages that talk more about aesthetics than outcomes.

## Design Principles

- Lead with evidence: outcomes, clients, channels, and operating context should be easy to scan.
- Keep the page compact: body copy should stay tight and useful, with generous rhythm only where it improves comprehension.
- Make typography do real work: Imbue carries editorial hierarchy; Plus Jakarta Sans carries precise body copy.
- Reduce template fingerprints: avoid repeated eyebrows, identical card grids, and decorative glass where plain structure is stronger.
- Protect the path to contact: primary actions should remain visible, legible, and reachable on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive states. Important content should be server-rendered and readable without JavaScript. Motion should be subtle, nonessential, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
